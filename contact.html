About Our Research

Our research is founded on Christuneocreatology™, an original interdisciplinary philosophical framework developed by Angel Naitbj that integrates philosophy, spirituality, science and technology into one unified system of knowledge for human advancement.

The framework recognizes that what many traditions describe as God, what science studies as energy, and what numerous spiritual traditions refer to as nature are different perspectives for understanding the same ultimate reality. Rather than separating these disciplines, Christuneocreatology seeks their integration to provide a more complete understanding of existence, innovation and civilization.

Angel Naitbj

ANGEL NAITBJ, legally known as Norbert April Igwe Theo Bar-Jehoshua Angel, is the founder of CHRISTUNEOCREATOLOGY™ and NAVENIE™, a Nigerian Systems Engineer, technologist, software and systems developer, interdisciplinary researcher, philosopher, and spiritual leader whose work spans science, philosophy, spirituality, engineering, and technology.

He is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of NAVENIE™ (NAITBJ ANGEL VENTURES INTEGRATED ENVIRONMENTS), an innovation and research-oriented organization dedicated to engineering systems, technological infrastructures, scientific research, and interdisciplinary innovation frameworks.

He is also the Founder and President of the UNIVERSITY OF CHRISTUNEOCREATOLOGY (UNIC), also known as the CENTRE FOR CHRISTUNEOCREATOLOGY STUDIES (CCS), structured around the CHRISTUNEOCREATIVE EDUCATIONAL SYSTEM (CES).

Angel Naitbj is also the Founder and President of the CHRISTUNEOCREATOLOGY HUMAN DEVELOPMENT AND INNOVATION FOUNDATION (CHDIF), also known as the "CHRISTUNEOCREATOLOGY FOUNDATION". The Foundation is a non-profit humanitarian and development-oriented organization established to promote human development, innovation and sustainable societal advancement.

Under his leadership, the CHRISTUNEOCREATOLOGY FOUNDATION serves as a platform for humanitarian engagement, empowerment, and developmental action aimed at improving lives and strengthening communities.

The mission of the Foundation is to advance human development and innovation through humanitarian service, empowerment programmes, creativity, ethical leadership, and sustainable development initiatives.

In addition, he is the Founder and Leader of the TABERNACLE FEAST OF NEW CREATION IN CHRIST MINISTRY INC. He is formally recognized as a Christuneocreatologist and Christuneocreative Philosopher.

CHRISTUNEOCREATOLOGY™ presents an integrative approach to knowledge that unifies philosophy, spirituality, science and technology into a structured civilizational model aimed at ethical innovation, human transformation and balanced development.

Angel Naitbj is the originator of the C777® CHRISTUNEOCREATOLOGY SYSTEM, a conceptual and philosophical framework organizing principles of consciousness, knowledge, intelligence and civilizational structure.

Professionally, Angel Naitbj is actively involved in software engineering, systems development, digital infrastructure design and technological innovation. His research connects systems architecture, knowledge engineering, philosophy of science, philosophy of technology and systems theory.

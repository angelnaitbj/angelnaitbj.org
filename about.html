Angel Naitbj

ANGEL NAITBJ, legally known as Norbert April Igwe Theo Bar-Jehoshua Angel, is the founder of CHRISTUNEOCREATOLOGY™ and NAVENIE™, a Nigerian Systems Engineer, technologist, software and systems developer, interdisciplinary researcher, philosopher, and spiritual leader whose work spans science, philosophy, spirituality, engineering, and technology.

The Call to Mount Maikunkele

As Angel Naitbj continued his sincere search for the ultimate purpose of existence, the origin of creation, and the destiny of humanity, his unending inquiry reached a defining moment. In response to his persistent pursuit of truth, his Lord spoke to him with a clear instruction:

"Go to the mountain and wait for Me, and I will give you the answer."

In faithful obedience to this divine instruction, Angel Naitbj ascended Mount Maikunkele in Minna, Niger State, Nigeria. He did not undertake this journey alone but was accompanied by his minister-friend and witness, Evang. Michael Olushola Olorunsogo.

The ascent to Mount Maikunkele marked a pivotal moment in his spiritual journey and carried profound significance. It demonstrated complete obedience to divine direction, a willingness to separate himself from the distractions of ordinary life in order to receive pure revelation, and the establishment of a credible witness so that the manifestation that followed would be verifiable and true.

The Supernatural Manifestation

While waiting upon the Lord on Mount Maikunkele, the heavens opened, and Angel Naitbj experienced a supernatural encounter that would define the course of his life and mission.

During this divine manifestation, he received the Illuminary Book; "The C777® Christuneocreatology System". This revelation was made known to him as the very purpose for which he was born—a comprehensive divine framework intended to restore creation toward the original design and purpose of the Source of Existence. It revealed a pathway for integrating Philosophy, Spirituality, Science, and Technology (PSST) in advancing human development, innovation, and the transformation of civilization.

The revelation was immediately accompanied by a visible confirmation from heaven. Two supernatural hands manifested upon him, serving as a divine sign affirming the authenticity of the commission entrusted to him.

This manifestation is understood within the framework of the biblical revelations recorded in Revelation 10 and Daniel 12. In Revelation 10, the mighty angel stands with one foot upon the sea and the other upon the land while holding the little book open, symbolizing the unveiling of divine mysteries and the commissioning of a heavenly mandate. In Daniel 12, the man clothed in linen raises both hands toward heaven, swearing by the Eternal One and signifying divine authority, appointed timing, and the confirmation of God's purpose.

For Angel Naitbj, these scriptural patterns represent the prophetic significance of the revelation received on Mount Maikunkele. The experience marked the beginning of the Christuneocreatological mission and the responsibility to proclaim, develop, and apply the revealed principles for the advancement of humanity and the establishment of a Christuneocreative Civilization.

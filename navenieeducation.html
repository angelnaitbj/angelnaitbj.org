NAVENIE™ EDUCATION PROCESS IS UNDER CONSTRUCTION

NAVENIE™ EDUCATION is an educational and knowledge development framework established by Angel Naitbj under the broader vision of science, technology, innovation, and human development.

The NAVENIE™ Education System focuses on developing individuals through integrated learning approaches that combine academic knowledge, technological skills, creativity, research, entrepreneurship, and practical problem-solving abilities.

It promotes education beyond traditional memorization by emphasizing understanding, innovation, critical thinking, systems thinking, and intelligent application of knowledge.

NAVENIE™ Education aims to prepare learners for the future by encouraging competence in areas such as:

• Science and Technology
• Software Engineering and Digital Systems
• Artificial Intelligence and Innovation
• Research and Development
• Entrepreneurship and Enterprise Creation
• Creative and Analytical Thinking

The goal of NAVENIE™ Education is to develop knowledgeable, creative, and responsible individuals capable of contributing positively to society through innovation and constructive solutions.

The NAVENIE™ Education platform is currently under development and will provide future learning resources, programmes, courses, and development opportunities.

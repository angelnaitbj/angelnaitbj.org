Angel Naitbj

Angel Naitbj, legally known as Norbert April Igwe Theo Bar-Jehoshua Angel, is a Systems Engineer, Software Developer, Philosopher, Researcher and Spiritual Leader.

He is the Founder of CHRISTUNEOCREATOLOGY™, NAVENIE™, the CHRISTUNEOCREATOLOGY HUMAN DEVELOPMENT AND INNOVATION FOUNDATION (CHDIF), and several innovation and educational initiatives.

His work integrates philosophy, spirituality, science, engineering, technology, artificial intelligence, innovation, and human development into one unified vision.
